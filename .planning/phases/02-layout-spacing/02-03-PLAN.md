Normalize max-width values across all templates to the three-value scale defined in SPAC-02: 900px (forms/detail), 1400px (lists), no constraint (calendars + dashboards). No !important changes happen here — those are Plan 02. This plan is pure max-width adjustments.

Output: All templates use only 900px, 1400px, or no max-width. Calendar templates lose their centering margin along with max-width (centering only makes sense with a constrained width).
